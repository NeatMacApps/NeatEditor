# NeatEditor Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 21, 2026

NeatEditor is a local-only plain-text editor developed by Zhichao Cao.

## Data Collection

NeatEditor does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, usage analytics, crash telemetry, or document content.

- All documents you create or open remain strictly on your device (or in folders you explicitly choose, such as iCloud Drive managed by Apple).
- NeatEditor does not require an account and makes no network connections in its Mac App Store distribution.
- Local preferences (theme, font size, language, tab state, and security-scoped folder/file bookmarks) are stored exclusively on your device via macOS system storage.

## Third-Party Services

The Mac App Store edition of NeatEditor includes no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or external AI services.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or NeatEditor, please contact:
- Email: support@caozc.com
- Support: https://github.com/NeatMacApps/NeatEditor/issues

# NeatEditor 隐私政策

**生效日期：** 2026 年 11 月 21 日

NeatEditor 是一款由 Zhichao Cao 开发的本地纯文本编辑器。

## 数据收集

NeatEditor **不会**收集、上传、出售或共享任何个人数据、使用统计、崩溃遥测或文档内容。

- 你创建或打开的所有文档均仅保存在你的设备本地（或你主动授权的文件夹，如由 Apple 管理的 iCloud 云盘目录中）。
- NeatEditor 无需注册账号，Mac App Store 版本不进行任何网络请求。
- 本地偏好设置（主题、字号、语言、标签页状态及用于沙盒持久化访问的安全作用域书签）仅保存在你的本机系统存储中。

## 第三方服务

Mac App Store 版 NeatEditor 不包含任何第三方统计、广告 SDK、追踪框架或外部 AI 服务。
